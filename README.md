# dheeraj

Inventory and financial management for a beverage and snack wholesaler. The proposed setup is a shared web app for the shop laptop and the owner's iPhone.

## First release scope

- Purchases and stock receipts, including free quantities and carton-to-unit conversions.
- Sales, stock deductions, and cash, UPI, and credit payments.
- Customer and supplier ledgers, including partial collections and payments.
- Returns, damage, expiry, and related stock and balance adjustments.
- Daily closing with collections, expenses, and outstanding balances.

## Decisions to confirm

- Laptop operating system and whether billing must work offline.
- Invoice format, printer requirements, and existing billing software.
- Loose-unit sales, customer-specific prices, and returnable bottles or crates.
- Which workflows the owner needs on the iPhone.
